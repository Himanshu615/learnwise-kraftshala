First, the simple version

Every time an ad appears, it counts.

An impression is counted each time your ad is shown on someone's screen. If the same person sees your ad three times, that's three impressions—not one.

Remember thisImpressions count ad appearances. They don't count how many different people saw the ad.

Let's make it real. Imagine your campaign is showing a new sneaker ad. Use the control to send the ad out a few times and watch the numbers change.

A different way to count

Reach counts people, not appearances.

Reach is the number of unique people who saw your ad. If Priya sees the same ad three times, she still counts as one person reached.

Remember thisImpressions = every appearance. Reach = unique people.

Try the same example again. The ad can appear many times, while the number of unique people stays smaller.
